## Users

- **Recruiters and hiring managers** screening web developer candidates. They usually scan quickly and compare several people side by side.
- **Freelance clients**, including SAP shops, looking for someone who can build web front ends that work with SAP systems.

## Product Purpose

Show both audiences that Evan is a strong web developer whose SAP knowledge sets them apart, then get them to make contact. Success means contact messages.

## Positioning

A web developer who also knows the SAP world: its terminology, systems, and constraints. Most web developers can't claim SAP depth, and most SAP consultants aren't web developers first.

## Operating Context

Recruiters skim and compare candidates in a short window. Clients judge credibility before they reach out. Both arrive with little time and decide on first impression.
