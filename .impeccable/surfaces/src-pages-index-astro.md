# Home (one-page site)

Mode: Persuade. Audience: recruiters screening web developers; HR teams and SAP partners needing SuccessFactors knowledge. Action: send a message through the badge's form. Proof: two Credly-verified SAP certifications, nothing else. No photo by choice.

Chosen sketch: `.impeccable/mocks/decision/assigned-badge.html` (code-led).

## Direction contract

THESIS: The page is Evan's staff badge on a lanyard; the four access zones are what he can be hired for. It refuses the default developer hero (headline, subcopy, button, row of skill cards).

OWN-WORLD: Graphite & Teal (Evan chose this on 2026-11-07, replacing the original lanyard orange because it was too loud, didn't feel like him, and he wanted a dark, calmer, more enterprise palette): graphite ground; one dark printed-PVC badge with rounded corners, a punched slot, and a steel clip; a slate woven strap printed in teal. Zone bands in light ink, teal, muted gold, and slate. One condensed face, display and text sizes only, ranked by weight and tracking. Exactly one reflective accent: the foil strip.

STORY: A visitor reads "web developer who knows how HR runs on SAP SuccessFactors", sees the four zones, verifies the certifications, and sends a message.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Left, one light ink line at display scale on graphite. Right, the badge hanging from a diagonal strap: EVAN KLINE / MORES as the largest type, position, four zone bands, and a working email + message form ending in Send. A barcode closes the card.

FORM: Staff ID badge, candidate 5 of 7. seed:8c586cadcfb4eae4. Signature interaction: the badge swings in once from its clip and settles; the foil strip follows the pointer. A sticky strap names the section in view. Zone states use line form (double rule on hover, dashed punch outline on focus, solid notch when current), never hue alone.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
